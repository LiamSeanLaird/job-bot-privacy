Job Bot privacy policy

Job Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, and it collects no data about website visitors.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail, read access: it searches the owner's mailbox for job alert emails from job platforms, and reads the matching emails.
  • Gmail, send access: it sends job notifications to the owner's own address only.
  • Google Sheets and Drive: it creates and updates one tracking spreadsheet, and can access only files it created.

How the data is used

Job details and the text of job alert emails are sent to OpenAI to extract job listings, identify duplicates and assess whether a job is relevant. Processing state is stored on a private server controlled by the owner.

The data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from their Google Account permissions, and can delete its stored data from the private server.
